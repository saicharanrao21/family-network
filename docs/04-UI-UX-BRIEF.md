# UI/UX Brief

## Design goal
Feel like discovering a living family universe, not filling a genealogy database.

## Visual language
Warm ivory, deep forest green, muted gold, charcoal, soft warm-gray. Rounded 20–28px surfaces, generous whitespace, photography-forward layouts, curved relationship lines, subtle motion.

## Primary navigation
Home | Tree | + | Search | Family

## Home
Family Universe summary, connected people, branches, generations, recent connections, memories, events and prompts to explore.

## Tree
Focus person, progressive expansion, depth controls, filters, zoom/pan, node bottom sheets and Explore Family action.

## Person profile
Photo, name variants, relationship to viewer, parents, siblings, unions, children, timeline, memories, privacy-aware details, Explore Family.

## Search
Large search field, natural-language suggestions, grouped results and relationship paths.

## Discover
Privacy-safe suggestions with an explicit “Why this was suggested”.

## Family
Family spaces, branches, memories, events, documents, roles and admins.

## Add flow
Progressive capture instead of giant forms. Relationship-first entry.

## Key interaction
Selecting a person recenters the graph and makes that person the new exploration focus. Branch expansion should feel fluid and spatial.

## Accessibility
Readable type, high contrast, semantic labels, touch targets ≥44px, reduced-motion support, screen-reader labels, color not used as the only relationship indicator.
